# Product context

EventOps Triage Lab teaches community organizers and new developers how to turn an ambiguous operational problem into a transparent, testable workflow.

Product principles:

- Explain every recommendation.
- Keep automated recommendations separate from human decisions.
- Use synthetic data by default and never transmit applicant data.
- Prefer a working, teachable slice over a feature-heavy prototype.
- Measure artifacts and completed actions, not vanity engagement.

The tone may be lightly humorous, but never dismissive of applicants or accessibility needs.
